# Image Review Checklist

## Purpose

- Is the image's role explicit: hero, background, illustration, poster, social visual, diagram, or asset?
- Does the image communicate the intended subject at final display size?
- Is the intended placement and aspect ratio known?

## Composition

- Is there one clear focal point?
- Is the crop suitable for the destination component?
- Is empty space intentional and in the correct location?
- Does the composition remain useful across expected crops?
- Is visual density appropriate to final display size?

## Direction

- Does the medium match the chosen visual language?
- Are palette, lighting, texture, geometry, and realism coherent?
- Is the image recognizably specific to the project?
- Are unrelated aesthetic trends excluded?

## Integration

- Does the image work with interface typography and colors?
- Does the interface respond to the image rather than placing it in a generic box?
- Is foreground-background contrast sufficient for any overlaid UI?
- Is a separate mobile crop needed?

## Artifact check

- No accidental generated text.
- No unwanted logos or signatures.
- No generic AI imagery unless explicitly required.
- No malformed hands, objects, geometry, or repeated elements.
- No inconsistent lighting or impossible material behavior.
- No obvious empty filler areas.

## Originality and reference safety

- Do not reproduce a protected composition.
- Do not imitate a living designer.
- Extract broad characteristics such as contrast, medium, crop, density, or geometry.
- Use owned, licensed, open, or generated assets appropriate for the project.
