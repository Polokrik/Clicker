# App Render Review Checklist

## Purpose and hierarchy

- Is the screen's primary job clear within the first viewport?
- Is the primary action visually and verbally obvious?
- Does the reading order match task priority?
- Are secondary actions genuinely secondary?

## Composition

- Does the first viewport read as one composition?
- Is there a clear focal point?
- Are scale, alignment, spacing, and contrast doing more work than containers?
- Is asymmetry deliberate rather than accidental?

## Density

- Is every visible element informative or interactive?
- Are decorative labels, icons, dividers, and helper lines necessary?
- Can any group be understood without its card?
- Are repeated components justified by repeated tasks or content?

## Typography and copy

- Are heading levels visually distinct?
- Are line lengths readable?
- Does text wrap well at target widths?
- Is centered text limited to short expressive content?
- Is the copy specific, concise, and human?
- Do buttons describe actions rather than vague outcomes?

## Color and surfaces

- Does contrast meet accessibility needs?
- Is the accent color reserved for priority, state, or action?
- Do borders, fills, and shadows communicate real layering?
- Are gradients and translucency conceptually justified?

## Components

- Are cards used for meaningful grouping or interaction?
- Are pills reserved for status or compact selection?
- Are icons useful without creating visual noise?
- Are controls large enough and states distinguishable?

## Imagery

- Does the visual anchor support the product or content?
- Does the image influence the composition?
- Is cropping intentional at all target sizes?
- Are placeholders and generated text absent?
- Is the image style coherent across the product?

## Responsive behavior

- Is mobile priority reconsidered rather than simply stacked?
- Does the primary action remain visible and usable?
- Are navigation and controls touch-friendly?
- Are wide components transformed appropriately?
- Is the small-screen first viewport still a coherent composition?

## Interaction and accessibility

- Are hover, focus, active, selected, disabled, loading, error, and empty states covered?
- Is focus visible?
- Can the experience be used by keyboard?
- Is motion tied to feedback or navigation?
- Is reduced motion supported?
- Is important information conveyed by more than color alone?

## Technical visual QA

- No clipping or overflow.
- No unintended horizontal scroll.
- No overlapping text or controls.
- No broken images or layout shifts.
- No illegible small text.
- No inconsistent radius, border, shadow, or icon treatment.
